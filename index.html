Breast Imaging ML · BCMID

Studying whether models can learn useful representations across ultrasound and mammography images without paired image translation.

  • Prepared patient-separated datasets and trained an unpaired ultrasound-to-mammography CycleGAN.
  • Compared direct ultrasound classification, frozen GAN image translation, and fusion baselines; examined class recall, macro F1, and shortcut sensitivity.
  • Experimental research only; synthetic images are not diagnostic evidence.
